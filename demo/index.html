      Why isolated is cheaper

      Jev pointwise feeds state + candidate as K separate sequences. Isolated packs them into one: each candidate (c1–c3) sees the state (s) and itself, never another candidate, and its position ids restart after the state. The logits are the same function, but the state is read once.

      Everything here is replayed from the saved test logits in the v0.1 release, after the validation-fitted temperature. No model runs in your browser. The users shown are drawn at random from four outcome groups (Jev right / SASRec right / both / neither), so they over-represent disagreements; the cards above give the true rates over the whole test set. "Auto" means the top probability clears the threshold at which that model's confident decisions are still 95% correct on the whole test set. Latency: one request, batch 1, NVIDIA B200, min of 60 interleaved runs.
